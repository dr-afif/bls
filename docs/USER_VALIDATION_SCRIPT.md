## Neutral Prompts
Use these prompts if the participant gets stuck. **Do not use leading prompts** (e.g., "Click on the Guides tab").
- "What are you looking for?"
- "What do you expect to happen?"
- "What are you thinking now?"
- "What would you try next?"
- "What does this label mean to you?"
- "What makes you say that?"
- "Please continue as you normally would."

## Introduction & Consent
"Thank you for taking the time to review this prototype with us. We are building a companion app to help run physical Basic Life Support (BLS) courses. Today, we want to see how intuitive the interface is for your specific role. 

This is an early frontend prototype, meaning the data you see is entirely fictional. **Please do not enter any real sensitive, medical, employment, or personal information.** 

As you go through the tasks, please 'think aloud'—tell me what you are looking at, what you expect to happen, and what you are trying to do. I cannot answer questions that reveal how to complete a task, but I am here to listen and take notes."

## Follow-up and Closing Questions

**Role-Specific:**
- (Learner) How did you find your physical course logistics? (If they mention a module progress bar: "Tell me more about what you expected.")
- (Instructor) What did the term "Teaching Kit" mean to you before you opened it? (If they mention Resources: "Tell me more about what you expected.")
- (Admin) How did you feel about the amount of information on the Overview screen? (If they mention density: "Tell me more about what you expected.")

**General/Open-Ended:**
1. **Terminology:** Were there any labels, buttons, or terms that confused you?
2. **Navigation:** Did you ever feel lost or unsure how to get back?
3. **Information Priority:** Was the most important information always easy to find on the screen?
4. **Density/Readability:** Did the screens feel too cluttered, or too sparse?
5. **Trust & Prototype Boundaries:** Were the boundaries of this fake prototype clear? Were you ever concerned about data privacy?
6. **Missing Information:** What essential information for your role was missing?
7. **Perception:** Does this feel like a companion app for a physical course, or does it still feel like a traditional online LMS?
8. **Summary:** What was the most confusing element? What was the most useful element?
9. **Final Request:** If you could change one thing about this interface, what would it be?
